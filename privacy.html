1. INTRODUCTION

PixiTales ("we," "our," or "us") provides an AI-powered storybook generation platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use your information when you register and use our services. We comply with global data protection standards, including the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO) of Hong Kong, as well as relevant principles of GDPR, COPPA, and APPI.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

To provide our services, we only collect the following minimal information:

  • Account Information: Email address and username provided during registration.
  • User-Generated Content: Text prompts, required story or page excerpts, reference images, annotation or crop images, and AI-generated stories and images that you submit or save.
  • Technical Data: IP address and device identifiers for security and performance monitoring.
  • Creative Draft and Sync Data: When you save a draft or continue editing a saved storybook, the story text, storybook language, target age, image aspect ratio, and related creation settings are synchronized with and stored on our servers to prevent data loss, restore your progress, and support continued editing across devices.

3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS & MEDIA ACCESS

  • Photo Library/Camera Access: With your permission, our App may access your device's photo library or camera. This access is strictly limited to two purposes: (a) allowing you to upload reference images for AI image modification features, and (b) saving generated images or storybooks directly to your device.
  • Image Processing and Storage: After your separate, explicit authorization, prompts, required story or page excerpts, reference images, annotation or crop images, and generated images needed for image features are transferred to mainland China and processed or stored by PixiTales, Volcano Engine TOS and Ark/Seedream, and, when selected, Qwen AI Platform/Qwen operated by Tongyi Yunqi (Hangzhou) Information Technology Co., Ltd. Original reference, annotation, and crop images are set to expire one day after upload. Object storage deletes them asynchronously, normally within 48 hours after upload; selected generated images are retained until the storybook or account is deleted. Unless we provide separate notice and obtain any authorization required by law, we do not use or authorize providers to use this data for model training.
  • User Responsibility: To protect your privacy, we strongly advise against uploading images that contain sensitive personal information, identifiable faces of minors, or confidential data. You assume full responsibility for the content of the images you choose to upload.

4. AGE REQUIREMENTS & PARENTAL CONSENT

While our App does not specifically request the age of its users, our content is designed for families.

  • Under 18: If you are under the age of 18 (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction), you must only use this App with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian.
  • Parental Control: We encourage parents to supervise their children's digital activities. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child without parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.

5. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

  • To manage your account and provide access to your generated stories.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates or support.
  • AI Processing: Story and text prompts may be processed by Volcano Engine Ark/Doubao. Image prompts and related images are processed, according to the selected model, by Volcano Engine Ark/Seedream or Qwen AI Platform/Qwen. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Account and business data is primarily stored in Singapore. Only after you give separate, explicit authorization before using image features are the required prompts, story or page excerpts, reference images, annotation or crop images, and generated images transferred to mainland China and processed or stored by PixiTales, Volcano Engine TOS and Ark/Seedream, and Qwen AI Platform/Qwen when selected. You may decline, but AI image generation and editing will then be unavailable. We apply contractual, notice, consent, and security safeguards required by applicable law.

7. DATA RETENTION & YOUR RIGHTS

Original reference, annotation, and crop images are set to expire one day after upload. Object storage deletes them asynchronously, normally within 48 hours after upload. Selected character, page, and cover images are retained until the related storybook or account is deleted. You may delete project content, withdraw image-processing authorization, or contact us to request access, correction, a copy, or deletion of your data. Complete removal may take a reasonable period because of backup and technical limitations.

8. CONTACT US

If you have questions about your privacy, please contact us at: xiaojie_gao@hkcsai.com.
